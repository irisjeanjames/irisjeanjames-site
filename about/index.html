About

I’ve been making things for print since I was 13, and I’ve spent 32+ years in design and 12+ in web development, keeping production websites online.

How I Work

A little creativity. A lot of curiosity. Absolutely no duct tape.

Okay, occasionally metaphorical duct tape. But only until I can build a proper solution.

I believe great digital experiences happen when design and technology work together, not when one is forced to accommodate the other.

Here’s what that looks like in practice.

Design is more than decoration.

My background in graphic design taught me to think about hierarchy, storytelling, accessibility, and how people actually experience information.

A beautiful interface is wonderful. A beautiful interface that makes sense? Even better.

Build it right. Then build it to last.

I love solving problems, but I’d rather not solve the same problem every Tuesday.

I focus on maintainable, adaptable solutions that can grow alongside the organizations using them.

Because today’s clever workaround shouldn’t become tomorrow’s emergency.

The server is part of the story.

I don’t stop at the front end.

Hosting, DNS, caching, backups, security, analytics, and performance are all part of the bigger picture.

If something breaks, I want to understand why. If something works, I want to know how to make it better.

And if something mysteriously fixes itself, I’m definitely going to investigate.

Curiosity is practically a job requirement.

I’m a firm believer in asking questions, exploring possibilities, and understanding the reasoning behind a solution.

I don’t need to know everything.

I do need to know how to find the answer, evaluate the options, and make an informed decision.

Preferably before touching production.

Knowledge is meant to be shared.

Documentation, training, and thoughtful handoffs are part of the job.

I want the people using the systems I build to feel confident managing them.

A successful project shouldn’t require a developer standing nearby with a magic wand.

In practice

  • I back up first. Before any change that is hard to undo, I take a copy of the database and the config, and I write down where it is.
  • I write it all down. Every job gets a write-up: the goal, before and after numbers, what changed, what is still open, and what I got wrong.
  • I fix things so they stay fixed. Small plugins and settings instead of edits to a theme, so an update doesn’t undo the work.

The Road So Far

From pixels to platforms. And everything in between.

My career started in print design, where I learned that typography matters, deadlines are real, and sometimes the smallest details make the biggest difference.

Eventually, my curiosity wandered beyond the page and into the code behind it.

One thing led to another.

Design became development. Development became infrastructure. Infrastructure became security, performance, accessibility, and an ever-expanding collection of interesting technical challenges.

Today, I work across the entire web ecosystem, combining creative thinking with the practical skills needed to build, manage, and improve production websites.

I still care deeply about how things look.

I just happen to care equally about what happens when nobody’s looking.

  1. October 2025 – Present

    Senior Web Developer & Systems Engineer

    VistaMedia, Inc. · Contract

    I manage the technical side of a portfolio of approximately 120 WordPress websites.

    That includes development, server administration, hosting, security, backups, performance optimization, accessibility, and the occasional production emergency.

    I’ve also built tools to audit website health across the portfolio, turning technical findings into actionable improvements.

    In short, I help keep a lot of moving parts moving in the right direction.

  2. January – October 2025

    Technical Account Manager

    Newspack, Automattic

    I worked with more than 300 news publishers, helping them navigate technical challenges across WordPress, integrations, analytics, and publishing workflows.

    With more than 500 issues resolved and a 95% customer satisfaction rating, I learned just how important it is to translate complicated technology into solutions people can actually use.

    Because even the best technical answer isn’t particularly helpful if nobody understands it.

  3. 2019 – January 2025

    Senior Web Developer & Systems Engineer

    VistaDigital Agency

    I started on the print side as lead designer, then moved to the web team and helped develop, maintain, and modernize more than 120 websites across publishing, tourism, hospitality, and entertainment.

    Highlights include building a WordPress multisite franchise network, delivering more than 60 accessibility-focused websites, and migrating 85 websites to Google Analytics 4.

    There was also plenty of hosting, troubleshooting, optimization, and figuring out why something worked perfectly yesterday but apparently changed its mind overnight.

  4. 2007 – 2016

    Art Director & Web Manager

    Dive News Network

    Before I was worrying about server configurations, I was worrying about kerning.

    I led creative and digital initiatives for a multi-publication media company, working across editorial design, branding, website management, and digital strategy. That included moving its websites from Drupal to Joomla while keeping the content and search visibility intact.

    Those years gave me a foundation in visual communication that still influences everything I build today.

    And yes, I still notice bad kerning.
