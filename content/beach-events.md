## Less searching. More celebrating.

Beach Events produces and promotes live entertainment in the Virginia Beach resort area as the city's primary live-events contractor. Their website helps visitors find what's happening, plan an outing, and get tickets. At least, that was the goal.

## The essentials
- **My role:** Helped on the original site, rebuilt it in 2021, and redesigned it in 2023
- **Client:** Beach Events, primary live-events contractor for the City of Virginia Beach
- **Built with:** WordPress and The Events Calendar (moved from EventOn in 2023)
- **Hosting:** hosting.com
- **Handoff:** Training materials for the events team

## The problem: the fun was too hard to find

The former site, LiveOnAtlantic.com, wasn't doing visitors many favors. About **85% of its traffic came from phones**, but navigation was cumbersome, ticket links were missing, and there wasn't a useful calendar. Finding an event felt a little too much like finding parking at the Oceanfront in July.

## Built around how people actually use it

I designed the experience mobile-first, putting the calendar at the center instead of making visitors dig for basic information.

- Responsive layouts for phones, tablets, and desktops
- An interactive calendar showing multiple events on the same day
- Event descriptions and direct ticket-purchase links
- Search optimization and the project's Level 1 ADA/accessibility requirements
- A simpler content-editing experience, supported by training materials

The goal was straightforward: help visitors get from *What's happening?* to *We're going!* with fewer taps.

## Good websites get another chapter

I helped on the original Beach Events site and did the full rebuild in **2021**, then came back in **2023** for another redesign that moved the calendar from EventOn to The Events Calendar plugin. The look evolved, but the calendar remained the star. Different design, same mission: make discovering events feel easy.
