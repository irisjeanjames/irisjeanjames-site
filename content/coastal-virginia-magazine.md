## From TownNews to WordPress
The magazine's website began on TownNews, a publishing platform now called BLOX Digital. I helped migrate over 20 years of data into WordPress. Later, I upgraded the site to modern WordPress from a prebuilt theme to a custom theme. 

## The monthly issue
A magazine lives on a schedule. I set up the templates and the issue bookcases that present each month's issue, and I made sure the issues went out every month.

## Advertising and search reporting
Ads pay for the magazine, so where they appear matters. I made sure advertising was placed correctly, and I manage the ads in AdRotate Pro. I also made sure SEO reporting went out for the site, so the team could see how it was doing in search.

## One brand, four sites
Coastal Virginia Magazine is the parent of three branded offshoots: the Wine Fest, the Burger Battle, and the Idea House. I linked the magazine's branding into the top header bar on each of them, so the four read as one family.

## The October 2026 audit
This is a large site with a media library of more than 12,000 images, and it scored a C+. The audit found that the page cache was switched off, so every visit rebuilt the page from scratch and took about 4.9 seconds to start loading. I turned the cache on, then fixed the page structure: one heading per page, a main-content area for the skip link, and article links that name the article they open. I darkened the brand teal so text meets the 4.5 to 1 contrast standard.

The biggest job was alt text. I added it to 12,035 images. An AI model drafted the descriptions from the pictures, I reviewed them, and I applied them through the database. The AI cost came to $0.83. A small upload rule now fills in alt text from clear file names, so new images don't add to the pile.
