Giving the keys back to the people who need them

The Virginia Beach Hotel Association supports its member hotels and helps visitors find their way around the city. Its website needs to serve two very different audiences: travelers planning a stay and hospitality professionals connecting with one another.

The problem: every little change needed a helping hand

The old website was outdated, difficult to use on a phone, and complicated to edit. Even routine content changes required outside vendor support, which meant more time and expense for something the association should have been able to handle itself.

That’s not a great arrangement for anyone with a budget. A rising tide lifts all ships; paying for every tiny update just sinks the budget.

A website the team can actually run

I helped build the original site in 2019, then rebuilt it on the same modern set of tested and proven WordPress plugins I use across my other sites. It has mobile-friendly layouts and practical tools for both audiences.

  • Advanced search to help people find what they need
  • Event sign-ups and job postings
  • Member directories and message boards
  • Search optimization and the accessibility requirements the project called for
  • An editing experience designed for association staff, not just developers

I followed up with one-on-one training and video tutorials, because handing someone a new website without showing them how it works isn’t much of a handoff.

Built for everyone who visits

Accessibility was part of the design from the start, not a pass at the end. The association’s accessibility requirements called for it, and I treated them as a floor. I shaped the layouts, headings, and navigation so travelers and members could find their way however they arrived, and the finished site met them. Good accessibility helps search visibility too.

The result: more independence, less friction

The association can now manage its own content. Visitors have a clearer path to planning a Virginia Beach trip, and members have a central place to connect.

A website should make people’s jobs easier. In this case, that included making sure they didn’t need me for every little thing.
