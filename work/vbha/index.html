Easier to find. Easier to add to.

The Virginia Beach Hotel Association supports its member hotels and helps visitors find their way around the city. Its website needs to serve two very different audiences: travelers planning a stay and hospitality professionals connecting with one another.

The quick check-in

Client
Virginia Beach Hotel Association

WordPress, with a member dashboard, search, event registration, job postings, and member directories
Members
Log in to add their own jobs and events

The problem: difficult to use and not intuitive

The old website was outdated, difficult to use on a phone, and hard to navigate. Information was buried deep within the site, which left visitors and member hotels frustrated and unable to find what they needed.

The new site: organized around what people came to do

I reorganized the site structure and rebuilt the navigation around what people are actually looking for: hotel information, events, and job opportunities.

Then I built a member dashboard. Member hotels log in and add their own jobs and events with ease.

Built for everyone who visits

Accessibility was part of the design from the start, not a pass at the end. The association’s accessibility requirements called for it, and I treated them as a floor. I shaped the layouts, headings, and navigation so travelers and members could find their way however they arrived, and the finished site met them. Good accessibility helps search visibility too.

The result: a clearer path in, an easier way to contribute

Visitors have a clearer path to planning a Virginia Beach trip, whether they’re looking for a hotel, an event, or a job. Member hotels have a central place to connect, and a dashboard where they add their own listings.

A website should make people’s jobs easier. Here, that meant organizing the information so it’s easy to find, and giving members a simple way to add their own.
