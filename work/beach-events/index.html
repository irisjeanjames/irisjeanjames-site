Less searching. More celebrating.

Beach Events produces and promotes live entertainment in the Virginia Beach resort area as the city’s primary live-events contractor. Their website has to help visitors find what’s happening, plan an outing, and get tickets. The old one didn’t always manage that.

The problem: the fun was too hard to find

The former site, LiveOnAtlantic.com, wasn’t doing visitors many favors. About 85% of its traffic came from phones, but navigation was cumbersome, ticket links were missing, and there wasn’t a useful calendar. Finding an event felt a little too much like finding parking at the Oceanfront in July.

Built around how people actually use it

I designed it for phones first, with the calendar at the center instead of making visitors dig for basic information.

  • Layouts that work on phones, tablets, and desktops
  • An interactive calendar showing multiple events on the same day
  • Event descriptions and direct ticket-purchase links
  • Search optimization and the accessibility requirements the project called for
  • A simpler content-editing experience, supported by training materials

The goal was straightforward: help visitors get from What’s happening? to We’re going! with fewer taps.

Good websites get another chapter

I helped on the original site and rebuilt it in 2021. In 2023 I came back for another redesign and switched the calendar software. The look evolved, but the calendar remained the star. Different design, same mission: make discovering events feel easy.
